About Me


Greetings! I'm Shami Islam Khan, hailing from the vibrant city of Dhaka, Bangladesh. My passion lies in crafting captivating websites. Currently, I'm pursuing my bachelor's degree in the Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) department at Brac University.

While I may not hold a formal certification in web design, my skill set extends far beyond. I specialize in projects involving Arduino, enabling me to fuse creativity with technology seamlessly. Additionally, I excel as a professional image and video editor, adept at crafting eye-catching posters and logos. My versatility extends to platforms such as YouTube, Instagram, and Facebook, where I've achieved notable success.

Patience is a virtue I hold dear in my work ethos. My dedication is evident in my unwavering attendance at college and in achieving the Guinness World Record after two years of tireless effort. Furthermore, I've clinched victories in numerous national and international competitions, shaping my journey with each challenge encountered.

In the face of adversity, I embrace it as an opportunity for growth, viewing every obstacle as a stepping stone towards success.
